# Database Integrity

Mechanisms that guarantee integrity and relationships between tables in [[sql]].

- **Primary & Foreign Keys (PK / FK)** — guarantee integrity and relationships between tables.
- **Constraints** — guarantee integrity but burden the DBMS, so used sparingly; most common is `NOT NULL`.
- **Sequences** — guarantee continuity and uniqueness; typically used as identifiers.
